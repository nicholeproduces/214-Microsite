Zoning and Development Potential — Please Read

Both parcels are zoned R-4B-C (conditional, rezoning case R449).

0 South Ave SE is a corner parcel, subject to setbacks on two street frontages rather than one. An architect who has built on this street calculates the as-of-right buildable envelope at roughly 11 ft x 90 ft — based on a 20 ft front setback, a 10 ft half-depth front on the Connally frontage, and 5 ft interior side and rear. That envelope is too narrow for a conventional single-family structure without a variance.

I’m not going to market this parcel as buildable as-of-right until the City confirms it in writing. A Zoning Verification Letter has been requested from the Office of Zoning & Development to establish the applicable setbacks, whether the parcel is a legal lot of record, and whether a variance would be required. It will be posted here when received.

Two paths a buyer should evaluate:

VARIANCE — Local precedent exists. A comparable constrained lot on Little Street SE received a variance in recent years, and two single-family homes were built nearby under variances V-22-122 and V-22-126.

CONSOLIDATION — City staff indicated administrative lot combination with 214 South Ave SE may be possible, creating roughly 52 ft of frontage on a single parcel. Under the current City ordinance, an accessory dwelling unit is permitted in R-4B on a lot with a single-family home — up to 1,000 SF, 24 ft in height, with 4 ft side and rear setbacks. Consolidating first may open a path the separate parcel does not.

Both paths are subject to City review and neither is guaranteed. Buyers must independently verify all zoning, setback, and development parameters with the City of Atlanta Office of Zoning & Development. No representation is made that the parcel is buildable as-of-right.

Tax Profile

Georgia law taxes property at 40% of fair market value — statewide, on every property. Following a sale, the county typically resets fair market value to the purchase price for the following tax year, so a buyer’s tax bill is based on what they pay, not on the seller’s prior assessment.
